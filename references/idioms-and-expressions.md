# Tigrinya Idioms, Discourse Markers, & Common Expressions

A reference for translating conversational phrases, idioms, transitions, and cultural expressions without resorting to English syntactic calques.

---

## 1. Greetings & Honorific Courtesy Formulas

| English Phrase | Tigrinya (Geʽez) | Transliteration | Register & Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hello / Greetings** | **ሰላም** / **ከመይ ኣለኻ፧** (m) / **ከመይ ኣለኺ፧** (f) | *Selam / Kemey aleka? / Kemey aleki?* | Standard everyday greeting |
| **How are you? (Respectful/Elders)** | **ከመይ ኣለኹም፧** | *Kemey alekum?* | Polite / honorific 2nd person |
| **Good morning** | **ከመይ ሓዲርካ፧** (m) / **ሓዲርኪ፧** (f) / **ሓዲርኩም፧** (pl/polite) | *Kemey hadirka? / hadirki? / hadirkum?* | "How did you spend the night?" |
| **Good evening** | **ከመይ ኣምሲኻ፧** (m) / **ኣምሲኺ፧** (f) / **ኣምሲኹም፧** (polite) | *Kemey amsixa? / amsixi? / amsixum?* | "How did you spend the evening?" |
| **Welcome** | **እንቋዕ ብደሓን መጻእካ** (m) / **መጻእኪ** (f) / **መጻእኩም** (polite) | *Inqwa' bidehan metsa'ka / metsa'ki / metsa'kum* | Standard welcoming formula |
| **Thank you** | **የቐንየለይ** | *Yeqenyeley* | Standard gratitude |
| **You're welcome / Don't mention it** | **ገንዘብካ** (m) / **ገንዘብኪ** (f) / **ገንዘብኩም** (polite) | *Genzebka / Genzebki / Genzebkum* | Equivalent to "It is yours / no problem" |
| **Excuse me / Pardon** | **ይቕሬታ** | *Yiqrieta* | Apology or seeking attention |
| **Goodbye** | **ደሓን ኩን** (m) / **ደሓን ኩኒ** (f) / **ደሓን ኩኑ** (polite) | *Dehan kun / Dehan kuni / Dehan kunu* | "Stay well" |

## 2. Discourse Markers & Logical Connectors (መረጋገጺታትን መተሓላለፍትን)

Natural Tigrinya relies heavily on particles and discourse markers to structure reasoning:

| Discourse Marker | Meaning / Function | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ስለዚ** (*Silezi*) | Therefore / Consequently | ስለዚ ሕጂ ክንነቅል ኣሎና። (*Therefore we must leave now.*) |
| **ይኹን እምበር** (*Yikun imber*) | However / Nevertheless | ፈቲኑ ነይሩ፤ ይኹን እምበር ኣይተዓወተን። (*He tried; however, he did not succeed.*) |
| **እሞ ደኣ** (*Imo d'a*) | "Then why / but then" (interrogative nuance) | እሞ ደኣ ስለምንታይ ዘይነገርካኒ፧ (*Then why didn't you tell me?*) |
| **ብፍላይ** (*Biflay*) | Especially / Particularly | ኩሎም መጺኦም፣ ብፍላይ ድማ ኣቦይ። (*All came, especially my father.*) |
| **ብሓቂ** (*Bihraqi*) | Truly / Indeed / In reality | ብሓቂ ዓቢ ነገር እዩ። (*It is truly a great thing.*) |
| **ብሓጺሩ** (*Bihatsiru*) | In short / Briefly | ብሓጺሩ እቲ ጉዳይ ተወዲኡ እዩ። (*In short, the matter is finished.*) |
| **ከምኡውን** (*Kem'uwin*) | Furthermore / In addition | ከምኡውን ካልኦት ሰባት ተሳቲፎም። (*Furthermore, other people participated.*) |

## 3. Idiomatic Expressions & Cultural Equivalents

Never translate idioms literally from English. Use authentic cultural equivalents:

| English Idiom | Literal Trap (DO NOT USE) | Authentic Tigrinya Equivalent | Explanation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Piece of cake"** | ❌ ቁራጽ ኬክ | ✅ **ቀሊል ነገር** / **ማይ ምስታይ** | Tigrinya equates ease to "drinking water" (*ማይ ምስታይ*). |
| **"Bite the bullet"** | ❌ ጠያይት ምሕያኽ | ✅ **ተዓጊስካ ምሕላፍ** / **ስኒ ምሕራቕ** | "Clenching teeth" or persevering through hardship. |
| **"Cost an arm and a leg"** | ❌ ኢድን እግርን ምኽፋል | ✅ **ክቡር ዋጋ ዘለዎ** / **ዋጋኡ ሰማይ ዝዓረገ** | "Its price has reached heaven". |
| **"Break a leg" (Good luck)** | ❌ እግርኻ ስበር | ✅ **ዕድል ይሃብካ** / **ሰናይ ዕድል** | Directly wish good fortune. |
| **"Keep in touch"** | ❌ ኣብ ምትንኻፍ ጽናሕ | ✅ **ኣይትጥፋእ** / **ንራኸብ** | "Do not disappear" or "let us meet". |
