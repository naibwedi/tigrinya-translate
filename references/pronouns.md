# Tigrinya Pronouns & Pronominal Affixes Reference

Consult this reference whenever handling pronouns, possession, vocatives, or verbal object markers.

---

## 1. Independent Personal Pronouns

Independent pronouns appear when the subject is contrasted, topic-shifted, or emphasized. In neutral statements, Tigrinya is **pro-drop** (the verb already carries the subject marker).

| Person | Familiar Singular | Polite Singular | Plural |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1st** | ኣነ (*anä* - "I") | — | ንሕና (*nəḥna* - "we") |
| **2nd Masc.** | ንስኻ (*nəsxa* - "you m.") | **ንስኹም** (*nəsxum*) | ንስኻትኩም (*nəsxatkum*) |
| **2nd Fem.** | ንስኺ (*nəsxi* - "you f.") | **ንስኽን** (*nəsxən*) | ንስኻትክን (*nəsxatkən*) |
| **3rd Masc.** | ንሱ (*nəsu* - "he") | **ንሶም** (*nəsom*) | ንሳቶም (*nəsatom*) |
| **3rd Fem.** | ንሳ (*nəsa* - "she") | **ንሰን** (*nəsän*) | ንሳተን (*nəsatän*) |

---

## 2. Vocative Markers (ጸዋዒ ቃላት)

Used to directly call or address someone:

* **ኣታ** (*ata*): Masculine singular vocative (e.g. *ኣታ ወዲ!* "Hey boy!")
* **ኣቲ** (*ati*): Feminine singular vocative (e.g. *ኣቲ ጓል!* "Hey girl!")
* **ኣቱም** (*atum*): Masculine/general plural vocative (e.g. *ኣቱም ሰባት!* "Hey people!")
* **ኣተን** (*atän*): Feminine plural vocative (e.g. *ኣተን ኣዋልድ!* "Hey girls!")

## 3. Possessive Suffixes on Nouns

Attach directly to the possessed noun. Note the vowel changes on vowel-ending nouns:

| Person | Suffix | On Consonant Stem: ከልቢ (*kälbi* "dog") | On Vowel Stem: ገዛ (*gäza* "house") | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1st Sing. | **-ይ** (*-y*) | ከልበይ (*kälbäy*) | ገዛይ (*gäzay*) | my |
| 2nd Sing. Masc. | **-ኻ** (*-xa*) | ከልብኻ (*kälbəxa*) | ገዛኻ (*gäzaxa*) | your (m.) |
| 2nd Sing. Fem. | **-ኺ** (*-xi*) | ከልብኺ (*kälbəxi*) | ገዛኺ (*gäzaxi*) | your (f.) |
| 3rd Sing. Masc. | **-ኡ** (*-u*) | ከልቡ (*kälbu*) | ገዝኡ (*gäz'u*) | his |
| 3rd Sing. Fem. | **-ኣ** (*-a*) | ከልባ (*kälba*) | ገዝኣ (*gäz'a*) | her |
| 1st Plural | **-ና** (*-na*) | ከልብና (*kälbəna*) | ገዛና (*gäzana*) | our |
| 2nd Plural Masc. | **-ኹም** (*-xum*) | ከልብኹም (*kälbəxum*) | ገዛኹም (*gäzaxum*) | your (pl. m.) / polite |
| 2nd Plural Fem. | **-ኽን** (*-xən*) | ከልብኽን (*kälbəxən*) | ገዛኽን (*gäzaxən*) | your (pl. f.) / polite |
| 3rd Plural Masc. | **-ኦም** (*-om*) | ከልቦም (*kälbom*) | ገዝኦም (*gäz'om*) | their (m.) / polite |
| 3rd Plural Fem. | **-አን** (*-än*) | ከልበን (*kälbän*) | ገዝአን (*gäz'än*) | their (f.) / polite |

## 4. Direct Object Suffixes on Verbs

Object pronouns fuse directly to the end of the conjugated verb stem:

| Object Person | Suffix after Consonant (Light) | Suffix after Vowel (Heavy/Geminated) | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Me** | -ኒ (*-ni*) | -ኒ (*-nni*) | ረኺቡኒ (*räxibu-ni* "he found me") |
| **You (m.)** | -ካ (*-ka*) | -ካ (*-kka*) | ረኺቡካ (*räxibu-ka* "he found you m.") |
| **You (f.)** | -ኺ (*-ki*) | -ኺ (*-kki*) | ረኺቡኺ (*räxibu-ki* "he found you f.") |
| **Him / It** | -ኦ (*-o*) | -ዎ (*-wo*) | ረኺቡዎ (*räxibu-wo* "he found him") |
| **Her / It** | -ኣ (*-a*) | -ዋ (*-wa*) | ረኺቡዋ (*räxibu-wa* "he found her") |
| **Us** | -ና (*-na*) | -ና (*-nna*) | ረኺቡና (*räxibu-na* "he found us") |
| **You (pl. m.)** | -ኹም (*-xum*) | -ኹም (*-kkum*) | ረኺቡኹም (*räxibu-xum* "he found you pl.") |
| **You (pl. f.)** | -ኽን (*-xən*) | -ኽን (*-kkən*) | ረኺቡኽን (*räxibu-xən* "he found you f. pl.") |
| **Them (m.)** | -ኦም (*-om*) | -ዎም (*-wom*) | ረኺቡዎም (*räxibu-wom* "he found them m.") |
| **Them (f.)** | -አን (*-än*) | -ወን (*-wän*) | ረኺቡወን (*räxibu-wän* "he found them f.") |

## 5. Prepositional Object Suffixes

Incorporate dative/benefactive ("to/for") or instrumental/locative ("with/by/in"):

### Dative / Benefactive Marker: -ለ- (*-lä-*)
* **ጸሓፈ** (*tṣäḥafä* - he wrote):
  * ጸሓፈ**ለይ** (*tṣäḥafäläy* = he wrote to me)
  * ጸሓፈ**ልካ** (*tṣäḥafälka* = he wrote to you m.)
  * ጸሓፈ**ልኪ** (*tṣäḥafälki* = he wrote to you f.)
  * ጸሓፈ**ሉ** (*tṣäḥafälu* = he wrote to him)
  * ጸሓፈ**ላ** (*tṣäḥafäla* = he wrote to her)
  * ጸሓፈ**ልና** (*tṣäḥafälna* = he wrote to us)
  * ጸሓፈ**ልኩም** (*tṣäḥafälxum* = he wrote to you pl. / polite)
  * ጸሓፈ**ሎም** (*tṣäḥafälom* = he wrote to them m. / polite)

### Instrumental / Locative Marker: -በ- (*-bä-*)
* ተጠቐመ**ሉ** (*täṭäqqämällu* = he used it / benefited by it).
