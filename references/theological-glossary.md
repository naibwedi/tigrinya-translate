# Tigrinya Orthodox Tewahedo Theological & Biblical Lexicon

A comprehensive reference for ecclesiastical, dogmatic, liturgical, and patristic translation between English and Tigrinya. Anchored in classical Geʽez roots and the historical theological literature of the Ethiopian and Eritrean Orthodox Tewahedo Church (*Haymanote Abew*, *Metsihafe Qiddase*, *Metsihafe Mistir*).

## 2. The Four Adverbs of Union (ኣርባዕተ ኣገባበ ተዋህዶ)

Classical patristic definitions governing the Hypostatic Union against historical heresies:

1. **Without Confusion (Inconfuse):** **ብዘይ ምትሕውዋስ** (*bəzäy mətəḥəwwas*) — Divinity and humanity do not merge into a third hybrid substance (contra Eutychianism).
2. **Without Change / Alteration (Immutabiliter):** **ብዘይ ምልዋጥ** (*bəzäy məlwaṭ*) — Divinity did not transform into flesh, nor was humanity swallowed into divinity.
3. **Without Separation (Inseparabiliter):** **ብዘይ ምፍላይ** (*bəzäy məflay*) — Divinity never departed from Christ's humanity, even on the Cross.
4. **Without Division (Indivise):** **ብዘይ ምኽፋል** (*bəzäy məkfal*) — The two aspects cannot be partitioned into two persons or actions (contra Nestorianism).

## 3. The Five Pillars of Mystery (ኣእማደ ምስጢር / ሓሙሽተ ምስጢራት)

The core theological curriculum taught in traditional Tewahedo church education:

1. **Mystery of the Trinity:** **ምስጢረ ሥላሴ** (*Məsṭirä Səllasie*) — One God in Three Persons (*ኣካል*) and one divine nature (*ባሕርይ*).
2. **Mystery of the Incarnation:** **ምስጢረ ሥጋዌ** (*Məsṭirä Səgawe*) — God the Word becoming man without cessation of divinity.
3. **Mystery of Baptism:** **ምስጢረ ጥምቀት** (*Məsṭirä Ṭəmqät*) — Rebirth through water and the Spirit.
4. **Mystery of the Eucharist:** **ምስጢረ ቍርባን** (*Məsṭirä Qwərban*) — True transformation into the real Body and Blood of Christ.
5. **Mystery of the Resurrection:** **ምስጢረ ትንሣኤ ሙታን** (*Məsṭirä Tənśa'e Mutan*) — Bodily resurrection on the Last Day.

## 4. The Seven Holy Sacraments (ሸውዓተ ምስጢራተ ቤተ ክርስቲያን)

1. **Baptism:** **ጥምቀት** (*Ṭəmqät*)
2. **Confirmation / Chrismation:** **ሜሮን** (*Meron / Məsṭirä Meron*)
3. **Holy Communion (Eucharist):** **ቍርባን** (*Qwərban*)
4. **Repentance & Confession:** **ንስሓ** (*Nəsəḥa*)
5. **Priesthood / Holy Orders:** **ክህነት** (*Kəhnät*)
6. **Holy Matrimony:** **ተክሊል** (*Täklil*)
7. **Unction of the Sick:** **ቀንዲል** (*Qändil*)

## 7. Strict Translation Directives for Religious Texts

1. **Lord's Name:** Always spell Jesus as **ኢየሱስ** (*Iyäsus*), never with phonetic variants.
2. **Church Fathers' Attribution:** Bold the full name of every Church Father wherever it appears (**ቅዱስ ቄርሎስ**, **ቅዱስ ኣትናቴዎስ**, **ቅዱስ ዮሓንስ ኣፈወርቅ**, **ቅዱስ ኤፍሬም ሶርያዊ**, **ቅዱስ ያዕቆብ ዘሥሩግ**).
3. **No Semantic Flattening:**
   - Translate *Incarnation* as **ሥጋዌ**, never as generic *ልደት* (birth).
   - Translate *Dispensation* as **ምድሓናዊ ሥጋዌ** or **መደብ ምድሓን** based on context.
   - Translate *Hypostatic Union* as **ተዋህዶ**.
   - Translate *Ascetic Discipline* as **ተጋድሎ** (*tägadlo*), keeping it distinguished from general monasticism unless specified.
4. **God the Word:** Use **እግዚአብሔር ቃል** when the source denotes the pre-existent or incarnate Logos. Retain **ቃል ኣምላኽ** when it means Scripture, a divine message, or God's spoken command.
5. **Incarnational Language:** Use **ብሥጋ ሰብ** only when the source means assuming or appearing in human flesh. Do not use it for an ordinary visible form or metaphor.
6. **Divine Intention:** Use **ኣብ ሓሳብ ኣምላኽ** for God's intention or eternal purpose; do not mechanically replace every occurrence of heart imagery.
