# Benchmark Translation Examples

These worked examples demonstrate correct grammatical decisions, authentic Eritrean Tigrinya phrasing, gemination marking in Latin transliteration, and appropriate register choices.

---

## 1. Definite Object, Pro-Drop, & Gerundive Past

### Example: "I ate the food."
* **Neutral Tigrinya:** **ነቲ መግቢ በሊዐ።**
* **Latin:** *näti mägbi bäliʿä.*
* **Linguistic Breakdown:**
  * **Pro-drop:** Omit independent pronoun **ኣነ** (*anä*) because the 1st person subject is already marked by the verb ending.
  * **Definite Direct Object:** "The food" requires the accusative prefix **ን-** fused with masculine article **እቲ** → **ነቲ** (*näti*). The noun for meal/prepared food is **መግቢ** (*mägbi*), never *እኽሊ* (grain/crops) or Amharic *ምግብ*.
  * **Main-Clause Past:** In standard Tigrinya, completed actions in a main clause naturally take the gerundive (here 1st person singular ends in 1st-order *-ä* **በሊዐ**, not 5th-order *በሊዔ*).
* **Emphatic Variant:** **ኣነ ነቲ መግቢ በሊዐ።** (*anä näti mägbi bäliʿä.*) — used only when contrasting "I" with someone else ("As for me, I ate the food").

## 2. Register Disambiguation ("You" in English)

English "you" obscures gender, number, and social distance. Tigrinya must resolve this before translating:

### Example: "Where are you going?"
* **Familiar Masculine (to peer/child/close male):**
  * Tigrinya: **ኣበይ ትኸይድ ኣለኻ፧**
  * Latin: *abäy təxäyyəd aläxa?*
* **Familiar Feminine (to peer/child/close female):**
  * Tigrinya: **ኣበይ ትኸይዲ ኣለኺ፧**
  * Latin: *abäy təxäyyədi aläxi?*
* **Polite / Honorific Singular (to elder, priest, teacher, stranger):**
  * Tigrinya: **ኣበይ ይኸዱ ኣለዉ፧** (or **ኣበይ ትኸዱ ኣለኹም፧**)
  * Latin: *abäy yəxädu aläwu?* (or *abäy təxädu aläxum?*)
  * *Note:* Uses plural morphology to show honorific respect to a single individual.

## 3. Sequential Actions & Gerundive Chaining

Never connect clauses mechanically with "and" (*-ን* or *እሞ*). Preceding actions take the gerundive:

### Example: "He came, ate his food, and went home."
* **Unnatural Calque (REJECT):** ❌ ንሱ መጸ እሞ መግቡ በልዐ እሞ ናብ ገዛ ከደ።
* **Natural Tigrinya (APPROVED):** **መጺኡ፣ መግቡ በሊዑ፣ ናብ ገዛ ኸደ።**
* **Latin:** *mäṣiʾu, mägbu bäliʿu, nab gäza xädä.*

---

## 4. Possession ("Have") and Obligation ("Must")

Tigrinya has no standalone verb "to have". It expresses possession using **ኣሎ** (*alo* = "there is") plus object suffixes:

### Example: "I have a book."
* **Tigrinya:** **መጽሓፍ ኣሎኒ።**
* **Latin:** *mätṣḥaf aloni.* (Literally: "A book exists to me.")

### Example: "You must come tomorrow."
* **Tigrinya:** **ጽባሕ ክትመጽእ ኣሎካ።** (m) / **ክትመጽኢ ኣሎኪ።** (f) / **ክትመጽኡ ኣሎኩም፧** (polite)
* **Latin:** *tṣəbaḥ kəttəmätṣʾə aloka.*

---

## 5. Yes/No Question with Particle "ዶ"

### Example: "Did you understand what I said?"
* **Tigrinya:** **ዝበልኩኻ ተረዲኡካዶ፧** (to male) / **ዝበልኩኺ ተረዲኡኪዶ፧** (to female)
* **Latin:** *zəbälkuxa tärädiʾukado?*
* **Structure:** Relative clause *ዝበልኩኻ* ("what I said to you") precedes verb *ተረዲኡካ* ("has it been understood by you") + question marker **-ዶ**.

---

## 6. Negation Contrast (Main vs Subordinate)

### Example (Main clause): "He did not call me."
* **Tigrinya:** **ኣይደወለለይን።**
* **Latin:** *ay-däwwäläläyn.* (Prefix **ኣይ-** + verb + **-ን**).

### Example (Subordinate clause): "Because he did not call me, I did not go."
* **Tigrinya:** **ስለ ዘይደወለለይ፣ ኣይከድኩን።**
* **Latin:** *səlä zäy-däwwäläläy, ay-xädkun.* (Subordinate uses **ዘይ-** prefix; final **-ን** is strictly dropped).
