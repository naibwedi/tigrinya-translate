# Tigrinya Grammar Paradigms & Morphosyntax

A reference of published Tigrinya morphological paradigms, agreement tables, and syntactic rules based on standard Tigrinya grammar (Amanuel Sahle 1998, Leslau 1941, Voigt 1977).

---

## 1. Personal Pronouns & Register

Independent pronouns appear when the subject is emphasized, contrasted, or topic-shifted. In neutral clauses, Tigrinya is **pro-drop** (the subject is fully encoded by the verb).

| Person | Familiar Singular | Polite / Honorific Singular | Plural |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1st** | ኣነ (*anä* - "I") | — | ንሕና (*nəḥna* - "we") |
| **2nd Masc.** | ንስኻ (*nəsxa*) | **ንስኹም** (*nəsxum*) | ንስኻትኩም (*nəsxatkum*) |
| **2nd Fem.** | ንስኺ (*nəsxi*) | **ንስኽን** (*nəsxən*) | ንስኻትክን (*nəsxatkən*) |
| **3rd Masc.** | ንሱ (*nəsu*) | **ንሶም** (*nəsom*) | ንሳቶም (*nəsatom*) |
| **3rd Fem.** | ንሳ (*nəsa*) | **ንሰን** (*nəsän*) | ንሳተን (*nəsatän*) |

> **Polite Rule:** Addressing an elder, stranger, priest, or dignitary requires polite singular pronouns (**ንስኹም** / **ንስኽን**) and plural verb agreement.

## 2. Canonical Verb Conjugation: √ፈለጠ (Type A: *fäläṭä* "to know")

Tigrinya verbs conjugate across four primary stems: **Perfective** (past/completed), **Imperfective** (present/future/habitual), **Gerundive** (converbal/completed main-clause past), and **Jussive/Imperative** (command/wish).

| Subject | Perfective (ሓላፊ) | Imperfective (ህሉው/መጻኢ) | Gerundive (ወሳኺ/ኅቡእ) | Jussive/Imperative (ትእዛዝ) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1st Sing. (ኣነ)** | ፈለጥኩ (*fäläṭku*) | እፈልጥ (*əfälləṭ*) | ፈሊጠ (*fäliṭä*) | ይፍለጥ (*yəfläṭ*) |
| **2nd Masc. (ንስኻ)** | ፈለጥካ (*fäläṭka*) | ትፈልጥ (*təfälləṭ*) | ፈሊጥካ (*fäliṭka*) | ፍለጥ (*fəläṭ*) |
| **2nd Fem. (ንስኺ)** | ፈለጥኪ (*fäläṭki*) | ትፈልጢ (*təfälləṭi*) | ፈሊጥኪ (*fäliṭki*) | ፍለጢ (*fəläṭi*) |
| **3rd Masc. (ንሱ)** | ፈለጠ (*fäläṭä*) | ይፈልጥ (*yəfälləṭ*) | ፈሊጡ (*fäliṭu*) | ይፍለጥ (*yəfläṭ*) |
| **3rd Fem. (ንሳ)** | ፈለጠት (*fäläṭät*) | ትፈልጥ (*təfälləṭ*) | ፈሊጣ (*fäliṭa*) | ትፍለጥ (*təfläṭ*) |
| **1st Plur. (ንሕና)** | ፈለጥና (*fäläṭna*) | ንፈልጥ (*nəfälləṭ*) | ፈሊጥና (*fäliṭna*) | ንፍለጥ (*nəfläṭ*) |
| **2nd Plur. M (ንስኻትኩም)** | ፈለጥኩም (*fäläṭkum*) | ትፈልጡ (*təfälləṭu*) | ፈሊጥኩም (*fäliṭkum*) | ፍለጡ (*fəläṭu*) |
| **2nd Plur. F (ንስኻትክን)** | ፈለጥክን (*fäläṭkən*) | ትፈልጣ (*təfälləṭa*) | ፈሊጥክን (*fäliṭkən*) | ፍለጣ (*fəläṭa*) |
| **3rd Plur. M (ንሳቶም)** | ፈለጡ (*fäläṭu*) | ይፈልጡ (*yəfälləṭu*) | ፈሊጦም (*fäliṭom*) | ይፍለጡ (*yəfläṭu*) |
| **3rd Plur. F (ንሳተን)** | ፈለጣ (*fäläṭa*) | ይፈልጣ (*yəfälləṭa*) | ፈሊጠን (*fäliṭän*) | ይፍለጣ (*yəfläṭa*) |

### The Gerundive in Main Clauses
In colloquial and standard written Tigrinya, the gerundive is the unmarked form for a completed single past event in a main clause:
- *ነቲ ወረ ሰሚዐ።* (*näti wärä sämiʿä.*) = "I heard the news."
- *መጺኡ።* (*mäṣiʾu.*) = "He has arrived / he came."

## 3. The Copula (እዩ) & Existence Verb (ኣሎ)

### The Copula ("To Be")
| Person | Present Affirmative | Present Negative | Past ("Was") |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1st Sing.** | እየ (*əyyä*) | ኣይኮንኩን (*aykonkun*) | ነይረ (*näyrä*) |
| **2nd Masc.** | ኢኻ (*ixa*) | ኣይኮንካን (*aykonkan*) | ነይርካ (*näyrka*) |
| **2nd Fem.** | ኢኺ (*ixi*) | ኣይኮንኪን (*aykonkin*) | ነይርኪ (*näyrki*) |
| **3rd Masc.** | እዩ (*əyyu*) | ኣይኮነን (*aykonen*) | ነይሩ (*näyru*) |
| **3rd Fem.** | እያ (*əyya*) | ኣይኮነMatchትን (*aykonätn*) | ነይራ (*näyra*) |
| **1st Plural** | ኢና (*ina*) | ኣይኮንናን (*aykonnan*) | ነይርና (*näyrna*) |
| **2nd Plural M** | ኢኹም (*ixum*) | ኣይኮንኩምን (*aykonkumn*) | ነይርኩም (*näyrkum*) |
| **2nd Plural F** | ኢኽን (*ixən*) | ኣይኮንክንን (*aykonkənn*) | ነይርክን (*näyrkən*) |
| **3rd Plural M** | እዮም (*əyyom*) | ኣይኮኑን (*aykonun*) | ነይሮም (*näyrom*) |
| **3rd Plural F** | እየን (*əyyän*) | ኣይኮናን (*aykonan*) | ነይረን (*näyrän*) |

### The Verb of Existence (ኣሎ - "To Exist / There Is")
- Affirmative: **ኣሎ** (*alo* - he is present / there is), **ኣላ** (*ala* - she is present), **ኣለዉ** (*aläwu* - they are present).
- Negative: **የልቦን** (*yälbon*) or **የለን** (*yälän*).
- **Possession ("Have"):** Fuses with object suffixes:
  - *ኣሎኒ* (*aloni* = I have)
  - *ኣሎካ* (*aloka* = you m. have)
  - *ኣሎኪ* (*aloki* = you f. have)
  - *ኣለዎ* (*aläwo* = he has)
  - *ኣለዋ* (*aläwa* = she has)
  - *ኣሎና* (*alona* = we have)
  - *ኣሎኩም* (*aloxum* = you pl. have)
  - *ኣለዎም* (*aläwom* = they have)

## 4. Nominal Affixes (Possessive & Accusative)

### Possessive Suffixes on Nouns (Example: ገዛ *gäza* "house")
- 1st Sing: **ገዛይ** (*gäzay* - my house)
- 2nd Masc: **ገዛኻ** (*gäzaxa* - your m. house)
- 2nd Fem: **ገዛኺ** (*gäzaxi* - your f. house)
- 3rd Masc: **ገዝኡ** (*gäz'u* - his house)
- 3rd Fem: **ገዝኣ** (*gäz'a* - her house)
- 1st Plural: **ገዛና** (*gäzana* - our house)
- 2nd Plural M: **ገዛኹም** (*gäzaxum* - your pl. m. house)
- 2nd Plural F: **ገዛኽን** (*gäzaxən* - your pl. f. house)
- 3rd Plural M: **ገዝኦም** (*gäz'om* - their m. house)
- 3rd Plural F: **ገዝአን** (*gäz'än* - their f. house)

### Definite Accusative: ን- Prefix
A definite direct object MUST be prefixed with **ን-**:
- *"The boy saw the teacher"* → እቲ ወዲ **ን**መምህር ርእይዎ። (*əti wäddi nə-mämhər rəʾyəwo.*)
- With definite article: **ነቲ** (*näti* masc.), **ነታ** (*näta* fem.), **ነቶም** (*nätom* pl. m.), **ነተን** (*nätän* pl. f.).
