# Quick Tigrinya Grammar Reference (for translation consistency)

## Word Order
- Default: **Subject – Object – Verb (SOV)**
- Example: "I ate the food" → ኣነ ንእኽሊ በሊዔ (not English SVO order)

## Verb Agreement
- Verbs must agree with the subject in:
  - Person (1st, 2nd, 3rd)
  - Number (singular / plural)
  - Gender (masculine / feminine) — especially important in 2nd and 3rd person

## Object Pronouns / Suffixes
- Object markers are typically attached to the verb.
- Possessive suffixes attach to nouns.

## Register
- Polite/respectful forms are the safe default for most interactions.
- Casual forms are appropriate among peers or when explicitly requested.
- Formal/written style leans toward more complete morphology and careful word choice.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid
- Copying English SVO order
- Ignoring gender agreement on verbs
- Over-literal translation of idioms
- Mixing formal and casual registers in the same sentence/document
