# Tigrinya Orthography, Homophones, & Punctuation Standard

A reference for maintaining orthographic consistency, correct fidel selection, and proper punctuation in written Tigrinya.

---

## 1. Punctuation Rules (ስርዓተ ነጥቢ)

Traditional Geʽez punctuation marks should be prioritized over Latin marks in formal and standard Tigrinya text:

| Mark | Name | Use Case | Latin Analog |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **።** | ዓራት ነጥቢ (*Arat Netbi*) | Closes a complete sentence. Never place a comma immediately before it. | Period (`.`) |
| **፣** | ንኡስ ነጥቢ (*Nius Netbi*) | Separates clauses, list items, or sequential gerund verbs. | Comma (`,`) |
| **፤** | ድርብ ሰረዝ (*Dirib Serez*) | Separates closely related independent clauses. | Semicolon (`;`) |
| **፦** | ነጥቢ መብርሂ (*Netbi Mibrihi*) | Precedes direct quotations, citations, or itemized lists. | Colon (`:`) |
| **፧** | ሕቶ ነጥቢ (*Hito Netbi*) | Concludes direct questions. Standard Latin `?` is also common in casual text. | Question mark (`?`) |

> **Formatting Note:** In digital typesetting, avoid double spacing before or after Geʽez punctuation marks. Ensure a single regular space follows `።` and `፣`.

## 2. Fidel Disambiguation & Homophone Guidelines (ተመሳሰልቲ ፊደላት)

In standard modern Tigrinya, certain historical Geʽez letters share phonological characteristics or phonetic overlap. Follow these standards:

### A. The S-Letters: ሰ vs ሠ
- **Modern Standard:** Tigrinya predominantly uses **ሰ** (*se*) for general vocabulary:
  - *ሰላም* (peace), *ሰብ* (person), *ሰበረ* (broke).
- **Ecclesiastical Exception:** Retain **ሠ** (*se*) in established ecclesiastical and theological terms derived directly from Geʽez:
  - **ሥጋዌ** (Incarnation), **ሥላሴ** (Trinity), **ሥርዓት** (order/rite).

### B. The Ts-Letters: ጸ vs ፀ
- **Modern Standard:** Tigrinya standard orthography uses **ጸ** (*tse*) for almost all words:
  - *ጸሎት* (prayer), *ጸሓፈ* (wrote), *ጸሓይ* (sun — commonly written *ጸሓይ* in modern Eritrean orthography).
- **Geʽez Liturgical:** Retain **ፀ** (*tse*) when quoting classical Geʽez liturgies:
  - **ዕፀ ጳጦስ** (Burning Bush), **ፀዋሪተ ፍሬ** (Bearer of Fruit).

### C. The Glottal vs Pharyngeal: አ vs ዐ
In Tigrinya, unlike modern Amharic, **አ** (glottal stop / *ʾä*) and **ዐ** (voiced pharyngeal fricative / *ʿä*) represent **distinct phonetic sounds** and convey completely different meanings:
- **ዓለም** (*‘Alem* = World) ≠ **ኣለም** (Mispelling)
- **ዕዮ** (*‘Iyo* = Work / task) ≠ **እዮ** (*Iyo* = Is / they are)
- **ዓይኒ** (*‘Ayni* = Eye) ≠ **ኣይኒ** (Mispelling)
- **ኣቦ** (*Abo* = Father) ≠ **ዓቦ** (Incorrect)

### D. The H-Letters: ሀ vs ሐ vs ኀ
- **ሀ** (*h*): Standard soft glottal fricative (*ሃገር* - country, *ሃበ* - gave).
- **ሐ** (*ḥ*): Voiceless pharyngeal fricative (*ሓቂ* - truth, *ሕጊ* - law, *ሓወ* - brother). This sound is distinct and strictly preserved.
- **ኀ** (*ḫ*): Archaic velar fricative; in modern standard Tigrinya orthography, words historically with ኀ are standardly written with **ሀ** or **ኸ** depending on regional phonology.

## 3. Standard Eritrean vs Tigray Dialectical Variations

When producing translation, default to **Standard Eritrean Tigrinya** unless a specific regional dialect is explicitly requested:

| Feature / Concept | Standard Eritrean Tigrinya | Tigray Regional Variant | Translation Guideline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Locative Preposition** | ኣብቲ (*abti*) | ኣብቲ / ናብቲ (*abti / nabti*) | Use **ኣብ** for location, **ናብ** for direction |
| **Adverb "Also / Too"** | ድማ / ኸኣ (*dma / xeka*) | ውን / እውን (*wən / əwən*) | Both valid; *ድማ* and *ኸኣ* preferred for formal prose |
| **Relative Past Marker** | ዝነበረ (*zinebere*) | ዝነበረ (*zinebere*) | Standard across both |
| **Spelling of "Now"** | ሕጂ (*hiji*) | ሕዚ (*hizi*) | Use **ሕጂ** in standard output |
| **Demonstratives** | እዚ / እቲ (*izi / iti*) | እዚ / እቲ (*izi / iti*) | Maintain standard forms |
