## 2. Core Syntactic Rules

### A. The Gerundive-as-Past Rule
In spoken and standard written Tigrinya, completed actions in a main clause naturally take the **gerundive** (*ወሳኺ ግሲ*), NOT the perfective:
- *"I ate the food"* → **ነቲ መግቢ በሊዐ።** (*näti mägbi bäliʿä.*) [1st-person gerundive ends in *-ä*, written with 1st-order letter **ዐ**, not 5th-order **ዔ**].
- *"He came"* → **መጺኡ።** (*mäṣi'u.*)

### B. Sequential Clause Chaining
Never connect sequential actions with English-style conjunctions ("and", *-ን*, *እሞ*). Preceding actions take the gerundive:
- *"He came, ate, and left"* → **መጺኡ፣ በሊዑ፣ ከደ።** (*mäṣi'u, bäli'u, xädä.*)

### C. Negation Patterns
1. **Main Clause:** Circumfix **ኣይ-…-ን**
   - *ኣይፈለጠን* (*ay-fäläṭän* = he did not know)
   - *ኣይፈልጥን እየ* (*ay-fälləṭən əyyä* = I do not know)
2. **Subordinate / Relative Clause:** Prefix **ዘይ-** (drop the final *-ን*)
   - *ዘይፈልጥ ሰብ* (*zäy-fälləṭ säb* = a person who does not know)
   - *ስለ ዘይመጸ* (*səlä zäy-mäṣ'ä* = because he did not come)

### D. Interrogative Particle ዶ
Yes/no questions append the enclitic particle **ዶ**:
- *"Did you know?"* → *ፈሊጥካዶ፧* (*fäliṭkado?*)

## 3. The Copula & Existence Verb

### Copula ("To Be")
* **Present:**
  * 1s: እየ (*əyyä*) | 2ms: ኢኻ (*ixa*) | 2fs: ኢኺ (*ixi*) | 3ms: እዩ (*əyyu*) | 3fs: እያ (*əyya*)
  * 1p: ኢና (*ina*) | 2pm: ኢኹም (*ixum*) | 2pf: ኢኽን (*ixən*) | 3pm: እዮም (*əyyom*) | 3pf: እየን (*əyyän*)
* **Present Negative:** ኣይኮነን (*ay-konän* "he is not"), ኣይኮንኩን (*ay-konkun* "I am not").
* **Past ("Was"):** ነይሩ (*näyru* "he was"), ነይረ (*näyrä* "I was").

### Existence Verb (ኣሎ) & Possession ("Have" / "Must")
Tigrinya has no standalone verb for "to have". It uses **ኣሎ** (*alo* = there is) plus object suffixes:
* **Possession ("Have"):**
  * *መጽሓፍ ኣሎኒ* (*mätṣḥaf aloni* = I have a book)
  * *ገዛ ኣለዎ* (*gäza aläwo* = he has a house)
* **Obligation ("Must"):** Prefix **ክ-** on imperfective verb + **ኣሎ-** with matching suffix:
  * *ክኸይድ ኣሎኒ* (*kə-xäyd aloni* = I must go)
  * *ክትመጽእ ኣሎካ* (*kət-təmätṣ'ə aloka* = you m. must come)

## 4. Laryngeal & Weak Root Notice
Verbs with root consonants containing laryngeals (**ሀ, ሐ, ዐ, አ**) or semivowels (**ወ, የ**) deviate from regular Type A vowel patterns (e.g. *በልዐ* "ate", *ሰመዐ* "heard", *ረኣየ* "saw").
> **Strict Directive:** Do not force the regular *fäläṭä* vowel pattern onto weak or laryngeal verbs. When uncertain of an irregular root's stem formation, verify it against Kane's dictionary.
