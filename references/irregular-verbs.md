# High-Frequency Irregular & Laryngeal Verbs

In standard Tigrinya, over 50% of clauses in natural speech and literature use weak, hollow, geminated, or laryngeal verbs rather than regular Type A (*√ፈለጠ*).

Do NOT force regular *fäläṭä* vowel patterns onto these verbs. Use this canonical reference table:

---

## 1. Top 8 High-Frequency Irregular Verbs

### 1. √መጸ (*mäṣ'ä* — to come)
* **Perfective (ሓላፊ):** መጸ (*mäṣ'ä* - he came), መጻእኩ (*mäṣa'ku* - I came), መጺኦም (*mäṣi'om* - they came)
* **Gerundive (ወሳኺ / completed past):**
  * 1s: መጺአ (*mäṣi'a*)
  * 2ms: መጺእካ (*mäṣi'ka*) | 2fs: መጺእኪ (*mäṣi'ki*)
  * 3ms: **መጺኡ** (*mäṣi'u*) | 3fs: **መጺኣ** (*mäṣi'a*)
  * 1p: መጺእና (*mäṣi'na*) | 2p: መጺእኩም (*mäṣi'kum*) | 3p: **መጺኦም** (*mäṣi'om*)
* **Imperfective (ህሉው/መጻኢ):** ይመጽእ (*yəmätṣ'ə* - he comes/will come), እመጽእ (*əmätṣ'ə* - I come)
* **Jussive (ትእዛዝ 3rd person):** ይምጻእ (*yəmṣa'*)
* **Imperative (ትእዛዝ - Suppletive root!):**
  * Masculine singular: **ናዓ** (*na'a* — "come here!")
  * Feminine singular: **ንዒ** (*nə'i* — "come here!")
  * Plural / Polite: **ንዑ** (*nə'u* — "come here!")

### 2. √ከደ (*xädä* — to go)
* **Perfective:** ከደ (*xädä* - he went), ከድኩ (*xädku* - I went)
* **Gerundive:**
  * 1s: ኬደ (*xedä*)
  * 2ms: ኬድካ (*xedka*) | 2fs: ኬድኪ (*xedki*)
  * 3ms: **ኸይዱ** (*xäydu*) | 3fs: **ኸይዳ** (*xäyda*)
  * 1p: ኬድና (*xedna*) | 2p: ኬድኩም (*xedkum*) | 3p: **ኸይዶም** (*xäydom*)
* **Imperfective:** ይኸይድ (*yəxäyyəd* - he goes), እኸይድ (*əxäyyəd* - I go)
* **Imperative:** ኺድ (*xid* m.) / ኺዲ (*xidi* f.) / ኺዱ (*xidu* pl./polite)

### 3. √ረኣየ (*rä'ayä* — to see)

* **Gerundive:**
  * 1s: ርእየ (*rə'yä*)
  * 2ms: ርኢኻ (*rə'ixa*) | 2fs: ርኢኺ (*rə'ixi*)
  * 3ms: **ርእዩ** (*rə'yu*) | 3fs: **ርእያ** (*rə'ya*)
  * 1p: ርኢና (*rə'ina*) | 2p: ርኢኹም (*rə'ixum*) | 3p: **ርእዮም** (*rə'yom*)
* **Imperfective:** ይርኢ (*yər'i* - he sees), እርኢ (*ər'i* - I see)
* **Jussive:** ይርኣይ (*yər'ay*)
* **Imperative:** ርአ (*rə'ä* m.) / ርኣዪ (*rə'ayi* f.) / ርኣዩ (*rə'ayu* pl.)

### 8. √በልዐ (*bäl'ä* — to eat)
* **Perfective:** በልዐ (*bäl'ä* - he ate), በላዕኩ (*bäla'ku* - I ate)
* **Gerundive (Crucial 1s spelling!):**
  * 1s: **በሊዐ** (*bäli'ä* — ends in 1st-order ዐ, NEVER 5th-order ዔ!)
  * 2ms: በሊዕካ (*bäli'ka*) | 2fs: በሊዕኪ (*bäli'ki*)
  * 3ms: **በሊዑ** (*bäli'u*) | 3fs: **በሊዓ** (*bäli'a*)
  * 1p: በሊዕና (*bäli'na*) | 2p: በሊዕኩም (*bäli'kum*) | 3p: **በሊዖም** (*bäli'om*)
* **Imperfective:** ይበልዕ (*yəbällə'*)
* **Imperative:** ብላዕ (*bəla'* m.) / ብልዒ (*bəl'i* f.) / ብልዑ (*bəl'u* pl.)

## 2. Derived Verb Stems (ግስታት ብስርዓት)

Tigrinya verbs alter meaning systematically through stem prefixes:

1. **Passive / Reflexive (ተ- prefix):**
   * √ሰብረ (*broke*) → **ተሰብረ** (*was broken*)
   * √ፈልጠ (*knew*) → **ተፈልጠ** (*was known / became famous*)
   * Imperfective passive: **ይስበር** (*yəssəbär*) / **ይፍለጥ** (*yəffäläṭ*)
2. **Causative (ኣ- prefix):**
   * √በልዐ (*ate*) → **ኣብልዐ** (*fed / made eat*)
   * √መጸ (*came*) → **ኣምጽአ** (*brought / caused to come*)
3. **Transitive / Intensive Causative (ኣስተ- prefix):**
   * √ማህረ (*learned*) → **ኣስተምሃረ** (*taught / instructed systematically*)
   * √ዋህደ (*was one*) → **ኣዋሃሃደ** (*integrated / united harmoniously*)
4. **Reciprocal (ተ- + 4th order 1st consonant):**
   * √ረኸበ (*found*) → **ተራኸቡ** (*met each other*)
   * √ቀተለ (*killed*) → **ተቓተሉ** (*fought / killed each other*)
